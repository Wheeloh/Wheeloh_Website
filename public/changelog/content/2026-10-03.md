# Wheeloh v1.3.0 — The Social Update
*03/10/2026*

We are thrilled to announce the release of our brand new version: **1.3.0**.

This new version introduces numerous exciting updates after three months of development! If you haven't already, be sure to update the app!

---

## Communities

Any Wheeloh user can now create public content accessible to everyone, everywhere. Whether it's automotive news, polls, posts, or albums... anything is possible!

### The user becomes a creator.

Are you a content creator on social media? Did you start a car club at your school or university? Or do you simply want to share your passion for the automotive world? Create your community today!

---

## Brand New Feed

On Wheeloh, two complementary feeds now work hand in hand.

### Private Feed

The private feed exclusively displays content published by your friends and the communities you have joined. It is sorted chronologically by publication date.

### Public Feed

The public feed showcases content from all users with a public profile across the app. An algorithm (currently in testing) curates content you'll love. It also features public posts from both joined and open communities.
